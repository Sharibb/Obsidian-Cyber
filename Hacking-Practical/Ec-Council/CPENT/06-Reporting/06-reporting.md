# Module: Reporting

Weighted equally with exploitation. Do not treat as an afterthought.

## Required Report Sections

- Executive summary (business-level risk narrative)
- Methodology (mapped to scope)
- Findings: title, evidence, reproduction steps, affected asset, severity
- Impact and risk narrative
- Remediation, prioritized and actionable
- Appendices: logs, commands, screenshots, proof of objectives

## Preparation Actions

- Build a reusable report template before starting technical study, not during exam prep crunch
- During every practice lab, capture screenshots and log commands as if writing the final client deliverable — build this as a habit, not a last-minute task
- Practice writing a condensed report from at least one full mock run before the real exam

## Timing

- Training material suggests 7 days from final session for submission
- Certification FAQ references a 30-day dashboard window with a paid 7-day extension option
- Plan conservatively for a 7-day submission target
