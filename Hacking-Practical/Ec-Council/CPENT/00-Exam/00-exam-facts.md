# CPENT Exam Facts

- 24hr practical exam, live cyber range, single 24hr sitting or split into two 12hr sessions
- Remote proctored, open-book (internet allowed, AI tools NOT allowed during exam)
- Scoring: 70%+ = CPENT. 90%+ = CPENT + LPT (Master), awarded from same exam, no separate test
- Mandatory pentest report submission after exam (target: within 7 days of final session)
- Voucher ~$999 USD, typically includes 90-day practice range access
- No mandatory prerequisites, open to direct purchase and scheduling
- Certification validity: 3 years, maintained via 120 ECE credits + annual CE fee

## Domains Covered

- Scoping and OSINT
- Perimeter assessment and evasion
- Linux/Windows exploitation and privilege escalation
- Active Directory attacks
- Pivoting and tunneling (including double pivot)
- Web, API, and JWT testing
- Reverse engineering, fuzzing, binary exploitation
- IoT/embedded testing
- Post-exploitation and data exfiltration
- Professional reporting
